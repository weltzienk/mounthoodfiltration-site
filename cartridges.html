Dust Collector Cartridge FAQ

Yes. We cross-reference OEM part numbers from Donaldson Torit®, Camfil Farr®, RoboVent®, Nordson®, AAF International®, UAS®, and many others. Submit your existing part number and we confirm the exact OD, length, pleat count, end-cap style, and media match—typically same business day.

Open/open cartridges have gasketed open ends on both top and bottom, used in continuous stacked assemblies or horizontal/vertical slide-in rack collectors. Open/closed cartridges have a gasketed open top and a solid closed bottom pan—usually with a centered bolt hole—for yoke-mount or single-tube-sheet collectors. The correct style is determined entirely by your collector’s tube-sheet mounting design.

For welding fume, plasma cutting, and metalworking, nanofiber flame-retardant media (MERV 15) is the gold standard—high efficiency on sub-micron particles and excellent pulse-jet cake release. Spunbond polyester suits coarser metalworking dust. For laser cutting and oil-mist environments, ePTFE membrane spunbond gives superior cake release. Processes generating flammable dusts or sparks should specify antistatic (conductive) media.

A 3-lug (or 4-lug) twist-lock cartridge uses a bayonet-style flange with locking ears spaced 120° (or 90°) apart. The cartridge is inserted into the tube-sheet and rotated a quarter-turn to lock—no tools required. This design is used primarily in Farr Tenkay® and Camfil® collectors, as well as some Nordson® and AAF® configurations. We build direct drop-in replacements in nanofiber, spunbond, and cellulose-blend media.

Yes. We supply BCSOVSB (spunbond) and BCSOVNF (nanofiber) oval cartridges in the standard 11.4”×14.4”×26” profile as well as custom oval dimensions. Oval cartridges use an expanded metal outer cage and wide-pleat design for maximum filter area in a compact footprint. Submit your collector model number or existing cartridge dimensions and we will confirm an exact match.
