Filter Bag Cage FAQ

Our filter bag cages are available in galvanized steel (standard 11-gauge for general dry-dust applications), carbon/mild steel, 304 stainless steel, and 316 stainless steel for high-moisture, corrosive-gas, and high-temperature environments. We also accommodate custom wire gauges and specialty coatings for abrasive or food-grade applications.

Yes—oval split cages are a core specialty. We build 2-piece and 3-piece split oval cages engineered for tight low-headroom plenums in hot-mix asphalt plant baghouses. We build to exact OEM dimensions for Astec®, CMI®, Gencor®, Standard Havens®, Barber-Green®, and other asphalt plant collectors. Send us your old cage, collector model number, or tube-sheet dimensions and we match the profile exactly.

A venturi is a converging-diverging nozzle at the top of a cage that improves pulse-jet cleaning efficiency by focusing the compressed-air pulse into a high-velocity jet that penetrates the full bag length. Whether you need a separate venturi depends on your tube-sheet design—some have a built-in venturi profile; others require a snap-in or crimped spun venturi on the cage top. We supply cage-only and cage-plus-venturi assemblies in spun aluminum, carbon steel, 304SS, and 316SS.

Exact dimensional matching is our standard, not an upgrade. We hold tolerances to ±1/32” on critical tube-sheet seating dimensions to ensure correct fitment. We cross-reference by OEM baghouse model number, existing cage sample, or tube-sheet bore measurements. Specify outside diameter, overall length, wire count (10, 12, or 20 vertical wires), and end construction (turned-flange top, bottom cup, snap-ring, or split collar) and we build exactly to your spec.

Top-load cages drop into the tube-sheet hole from the clean-air plenum side; a flange or collar seats on the tube-sheet surface and the filter bag mounts over the cage from above. Snap-ring cages use a spring-steel locking ring to secure the bag to the cage top at the tube-sheet. Envelope cages (flat or pocket cages) support flat or multi-pocket filter bags in shaker and reverse-air collectors with flat tube-sheets. We build all three types to custom or OEM specifications.
