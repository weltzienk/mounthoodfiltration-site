Pulse-jet bags use a compressed-air pulse to knock dust off the outside of the bag; they need a wire cage for support and have snap-band, flange, or ring tops. Shaker bags are cleaned by mechanical vibration and use loop, strap, or grommet tops—no cage required. Reverse-air bags are cleaned by temporarily reversing airflow and require internal anti-collapse rings at intervals to prevent inward buckling. We manufacture all three types in any media to exact OEM specifications.

Common high-temperature media: Nomex® (Aramid) to 400°F—asphalt plants, foundries, cement kilns. PTFE/Teflon® & woven fiberglass to 500°F—boilers, incinerators, chemical processing. P84® polyimide to 500°F—extreme chemical and sub-micron environments. PPS/Ryton® to 375°F—acidic gas streams. For standard dry-dust applications under 275°F, polyester felt is the cost-effective baseline. Not sure?
