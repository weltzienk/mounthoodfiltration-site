Field Documentation // Filter Cage Installation

Technician demonstrates precise oval cage seating technique inside a pulse-jet baghouse — using a wooden board for stability and controlled foot pressure to seat cages without dislodging filter bags.

Why Technique Matters
  • 01 Incorrect cage seating is the leading cause of bag blowouts during the first pulse cycle after a change-out.
  • 02 Oval cages require a specific rotational orientation to clear the tubesheet opening — force indicates misalignment, not resistance.
  • 03 A properly seated cage should drop fully under its own weight once aligned. Foot pressure is a final-seating assist only.
